Problem Solution Essay

Analyzing and Writing Problem-Solution Essays

This session introduced the problem-solution essay format. I learned that this type of essay clearly states a problem, explains why it matters, and then proposes practical solutions supported by evidence. Like the compare and contrast essay, it can also be written in block style (all problems first, then solutions) or chain style (each problem paired with its solution). We analyzed a model essay and practiced identifying the structure of the introduction, body paragraphs, conclusion, and the transition words used. This was useful because problem-solution thinking is not just academic. It is how people approach real decisions at work and in daily life. The hardest part was making sure my solutions were actually supported by evidence and not just opinions. It is easy to say "the government should do X" but harder to prove that X actually works. I improved by looking up real examples from other countries and using those as evidence instead of just stating what I think should happen.

Ezechiel Irankunda - Kepler College SNHU26 - English Communications 2
