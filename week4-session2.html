Cause and Effect Essay

SESSION 2: Writing a Cause and Effect Essay

In the final session of this phase, we worked on cause and effect essays. This type of essay explains why something happens (the causes) and what results from it (the effects). Like the other essay types, it can be structured using block or chain (point-by-point) style. We analyzed a sample essay in groups to identify how the introduction, body paragraphs, and conclusion are structured, then practiced writing our own essays on topics like procrastination and job burnout. This was important because understanding cause and effect is how you build strong arguments in any field. If you can explain why a problem exists, you are already halfway to solving it. The challenge I faced was separating causes from effects because sometimes they overlap. For example, stress can be both a cause of procrastination and an effect of it. I dealt with this by being very specific about the direction: in my essay, I treated stress as an effect and focused on poor time management and fear of failure as the clear causes.
