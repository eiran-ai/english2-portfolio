Paragraph Structure: CER/CLEAR

SESSION 1: Building Strong Paragraphs with CER and CLEAR

This week focused on how to write a strong academic paragraph. We learned two frameworks: CER (Claim, Evidence, Reasoning) and CLEAR (Claim, Link, Evidence, Analysis, Review). Both give you a step-by-step way to organize a single paragraph so that it makes a clear point and supports it properly. This was important because a paragraph is the building block of every essay. If your paragraphs are messy, the whole essay falls apart. I also learned the difference between different types of paragraphs like descriptive, narrative, expository, and persuasive. The biggest challenge for me was the CLEAR framework because it has more steps than CER and I kept forgetting the "Link" part. The link connects your claim to the previous paragraph or to the bigger topic, and without it, the paragraph feels disconnected. I fixed this by practicing on short prompts and always writing the link sentence right after the topic sentence before moving on.
