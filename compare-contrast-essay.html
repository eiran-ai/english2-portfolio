Compare and Contrast Essay

Writing a Compare and Contrast Essay

This week introduced a new essay type: the compare and contrast essay. I learned two organizational styles for writing it: block style (all points about subject A first, then all about subject B) and chain style (point by point, alternating between A and B). We also studied transition words used specifically for comparison like "similarly" and "likewise" and for contrast like "however" and "on the other hand." We practiced by analyzing sample essays and then writing our own. This was important because being able to compare and contrast is a skill used everywhere, from choosing between two products to evaluating different solutions to a problem. The challenge I faced was choosing between block and chain style. I started with block but realized halfway through that my essay felt disconnected because the reader had to remember details from the first section while reading the second. I switched to chain style for my final draft and it flowed much better because each paragraph handled one point of comparison directly.
