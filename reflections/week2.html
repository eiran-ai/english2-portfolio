Week 2 Reflection

Session 1: Paragraph Structure - CER and CLEAR

This week we learned how to build a proper academic paragraph using two frameworks. CER stands for Claim, Evidence, Reasoning. CLEAR adds two more steps: Link and Review. Before this, my paragraphs were just a bunch of sentences about the same topic. I didn't really think about whether each sentence had a specific job. The CER framework made it click for me. You state what you believe, you back it up with facts, and then you explain why the facts matter. That last part, the reasoning, is what I kept skipping before. I'd just drop a quote and move on. Now I know the explanation after the evidence is actually the most important part because that's where your own thinking shows.

Session 2: Informative Essay - Outline and Introduction

We started working on informative essays in this session. The first thing we did was learn how to build an outline before writing anything. I used to just start typing and hope the essay would come together. That never worked well. An outline forces you to know your main points before you start writing, so you don't go in random directions. We also broke down the introduction into three parts: hook, background information, and thesis statement. The hook is supposed to grab the reader's attention. My first try was something like "This essay is about..." which my instructor said is not a hook at all. I switched to a surprising statistic and it worked much better. The thesis was also hard because it has to preview everything the essay will cover in one sentence.

Ezechiel Irankunda - Kepler College SNHU26 - English Communications 2
