Week 1 Reflection

Session 1: APA In-text Citations

This was my first real introduction to APA citation. Before this class, I thought citing sources just meant putting a link at the bottom of your paper. Turns out it's way more specific than that. We learned two styles: parenthetical, where you put the author and year in brackets at the end of a sentence, and narrative, where you work the author's name into the sentence itself. The part that tripped me up was knowing when to include page numbers. Direct quotes need them, but paraphrases don't always. I made myself a small cheat sheet on my laptop so I can check quickly when I'm writing. We also talked about plagiarism and why it's taken so seriously at Kepler. That part was a wake-up call because I realized some things I did in secondary school would actually count as plagiarism here.

Session 2: APA Reference List

Session 2 was about building a full reference list at the end of a paper. We practiced writing entries for books, journal articles, websites, YouTube videos, and even ChatGPT. Each source type has its own format, and honestly it felt overwhelming at first. The journal article format was the hardest because there are so many parts: author, year, title, journal name, volume, issue, pages. I kept mixing up which parts to italicize. What helped me was treating the APA guide like a template. I'd open it next to my document and fill in each field one at a time instead of trying to remember the whole thing from memory. By the end of the session I could write a reference entry for a website without looking at the guide, so that felt like progress.

Ezechiel Irankunda - Kepler College SNHU26 - English Communications 2
